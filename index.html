Safety

Read this before anyone messages you.

The most common attack on new token holders is not a contract exploit. It is somebody pretending to be us.

  • ✕

    Your seed phrase is the wallet

    Anyone who has it controls your funds. Not us, not anyone. Never type it into a website, a form, or a DM — not even one that looks official.

  • ✕

    Staff will never ask for it

    Not the admins, not @TokumiHQBot, not anyone claiming to help. If a message asks for a seed phrase, a private key, or your 2FA code, it is a scam.

  • ✕

    Confirm the handle

    Our channel is @TokumiOfficial. Look for the exact name. Report impersonators in-channel and the mods will remove them.

  • ✕

    Nobody here gives financial advice

    Members discuss the token. That is not advice, and nobody speaking here is telling you what to buy. Anything that sounds like a tip is worth distrusting.
